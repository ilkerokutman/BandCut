# Privacy Policy for BandCut

**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

BandCut is a macOS application developed by Performans Iletisim ve Tek. Hiz. Ltd. Sti. This policy explains how BandCut handles information.

## Data collection

BandCut does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use personal data for tracking. The app does not include user accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Audio files and local processing

BandCut accesses WAV files only when the user selects them through the macOS file picker. Audio analysis, waveform generation, playback, track editing, and MP3 export are performed locally on the user's Mac. Audio files, track names, project names, and exported MP3 files are not uploaded to BandCut or Performans servers.

Exported MP3 files are written only to a directory selected by the user.

## Local settings

BandCut stores track-detection preferences, such as the silence threshold and minimum track duration, in the app's local application-support container. These settings remain on the user's Mac and can be removed by deleting the app's local data.

## Network access

BandCut does not require an internet connection for its audio-processing features and does not transmit app usage or audio content over the internet.

## Third-party software

BandCut includes FFmpeg and LAME for local audio conversion. These components process files locally and are bundled without network functionality. Their licenses and source information are included with the application and in the BandCut source repository.

## Children's privacy

BandCut does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if BandCut's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
